# Barely An Expert — topic bank

100 ideas, 20 per script template. Type the title into the composer **exactly as written**: the
script stage pins the video title to the topic verbatim.

Each carries an **anchor** — the real case, study or number the script should be built on. Anchors
exist so the writer starts somewhere true; they are **not a guarantee**. The description stage
verifies citations against Crossref and Open Library, but it cannot check a date you asserted in
narration. Check anything load-bearing before it ships.

## Title style, taken from the reference's 118 videos

- **Only 10 of his 118 titles start with a number**, even though every video is a countdown of ten.
  So the titles below don't either. The "ten" lives in the script, not the title.
- The grammar is almost always one of: `[Plural Noun] That [Verb Clause]`,
  `The [Superlative] [Noun] [Prepositional Phrase]`, or `Why [Statement]`.
- Title Case throughout. No colons, no brackets, no "You Won't Believe".
- **None of these duplicate his existing 118.** His catalogue is in
  `_research/some-guy-who-knows-stuff/titles.txt` — check it before adding more.
- His channel drifted over time: the oldest videos are gross-out listicles ("Medieval Hygiene
  Habits That Will Make You Gag"), the newest are science and history mysteries. **These aim at
  the newer register**, which is where his audience is now.
