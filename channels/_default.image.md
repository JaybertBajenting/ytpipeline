Follow this design plan STRICTLY for every image. Do not deviate from the style or the rules below.

You are generating images for a YouTube script. Read the script carefully and create visual frames that match the narration.

ONE IMAGE PER BEAT (IMPORTANT):
The script has already been broken into short, one-idea beats for a fast-paced edit. Generate
EXACTLY ONE image per beat — do NOT split a beat into multiple images, and do NOT merge beats.
Produce exactly as many images as there are beats. The video editor times each image to the
exact words of its beat, so one image per beat is what keeps the visuals locked to the narration.

Each image must visually illustrate what the narrator is saying during that beat. It should make sense with the story, the emotion, and the idea being explained. Do not create random images.

STYLE REQUIREMENTS (STRICT):
The image style must look like extremely simple beginner drawings made in MS Paint. It should look like someone who is not good at drawing created it quickly by hand.

Use a very simple stickman / childish drawing style:
- White background
- Thick, uneven black outlines
- Wobbly hand-drawn lines
- Stick figure humans with round heads and line bodies
- Simple dot eyes or circle eyes
- Very basic facial expressions
- Flat colors only
- No realistic shading
- No 3D
- No cinematic lighting
- No realistic cartoon style
- No Disney style
- No anime style
- No polished illustration style
- No professional vector art
- No highly detailed backgrounds
- No complex textures
- No realistic humans
- No glossy or modern design

The drawings should feel amateur, funny, simple, and intentionally “bad,” like a noob drew them in Paint.

Objects should be drawn with basic shapes: squares, circles, rectangles, arrows, simple tables, boxes, trees, rooms, signs, screens, stickmen, question marks, simple symbols.

Use the same visual language throughout:
- Simple black line drawings
- Mostly white empty space
- Occasional flat colors like green, brown, gray, red, yellow, orange, and blue
- Red arrows or red question marks when needed
- Handwritten text only when it helps explain the idea

TEXT RULES:
If text appears in the image: keep it very short, spell it correctly, make it easy to read, do not add long sentences, do not put too many labels in one frame.

FORMAT REQUIREMENTS:
Every image must be horizontal 16:9 for YouTube. Clean and readable, centered composition, do not crop important objects, leave enough space around characters and objects. Not vertical, not square.

Avoid: glitches, broken anatomy, unreadable text, messy overlapping objects, weird extra details, images that look too polished, images that look professional.
