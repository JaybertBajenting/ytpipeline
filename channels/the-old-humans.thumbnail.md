# Thumbnail rule — Ancient Us

Follow this STRICTLY. It deliberately OVERRIDES the shared white-background thumbnail rule: this
channel's thumbnails are full-bleed painted scenes, not white cards.

A thumbnail is not a frame from the video. A frame explains; a thumbnail sells one situation. It
has to read at roughly 320 pixels wide, in a crowded feed, in under a second.

## Canvas

- Horizontal 16:9, **exactly 1280x720**.
- The artwork is **full bleed**: the painted scene runs to all four edges and into all four
  corners. No white background, no card, no rounded corners, no drop shadow, no border, no
  letterboxing, no browser or player chrome, no "YouTube thumbnail" mockup.
- Deliver the thumbnail itself, not a picture of a thumbnail.

## The scene

One situation, drawn as a painted environment with real depth and atmosphere — a firelit cave in
a rainstorm, a snowbound shelter, a savanna at dusk, a river crossing, a torchlit temple.

- One to three characters, close enough that their faces are readable at 320 pixels wide.
- Characters follow the channel character bible exactly: thin black stick limbs, big ivory round
  heads, black oval eyes with eyelid lines, expressive brows, scribbled brown hair, spotted-hide
  tunics. Flat characters inside a shaded world.
- Give them ONE clear readable emotion: exhausted, smug, terrified, content, drunk, baffled.
- The signature contrast: a warm orange firelit subject against a cold blue-grey or dark
  surroundings. Warm inside, hostile outside.
- Include one prop that names the topic instantly — a fire, a mammoth, a stone cup, a spear, a
  snowdrift, a rain sheet.

## Text

- **Two to four words. ALL CAPS. Nothing longer.** Examples of the right register:
  RAINED ALL WEEK · COZY INSIDE? · DRINKS ALL DAY · NO JOBS · BORN TO KILL? · FIRST GUN? · WHY US?
- Heavy rounded or condensed bold sans, set across the **top fifth of the frame, edge to edge**.
- Pure white or bright yellow, with a **thick black outline** (roughly 8-10 px at 1280 wide) so it
  survives any background behind it.
- It is a punchy fragment of the title, never the full title, never a sentence.
- Question mark only when the words genuinely ask something.
- Spell it correctly and make it huge. If it has to shrink to fit, cut a word.

## Optional accents — at most ONE per thumbnail

- A single thick white arrow pointing at the subject.
- A red question mark or red circle on the one thing that matters.

Never both. Never more than one of either.

## Hard bans

- No white or plain background — this channel's thumbnails are always a scene.
- No photorealism, no stock photos, no cinematic 3D, no anime or Disney styling.
- No channel name, no logo, no watermark, no subscribe button, no face-cam circle.
- No real people, no celebrities, no brand marks.
- No gore, no explicit wounds, no sexualised figures.
- No small print, no second line of text, no caption bar, no borders or frames.
- No arrow spam, no giant red circles around everything — one accent, maximum.

## Test before finishing

1. Do the artwork and colour run into all four corners, with no card, bar or caption anywhere?
2. Shrink it mentally to a small box in a crowded feed: can you tell the situation from the shapes
   and the faces alone, and read every word of the text?
3. Is the text two to four words, all caps, outlined, across the top?
