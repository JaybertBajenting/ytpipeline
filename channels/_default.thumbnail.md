# Thumbnail rule — shared by every channel

Follow this STRICTLY. A thumbnail is not a frame from the video: frames explain, thumbnails
provoke. It has to read as a single idea at roughly 320 pixels wide, in a crowded feed.

## Medium

Same hand-drawn MS Paint look as the video frames — thick, uneven black outlines, wobbly
hand-drawn lines, flat solid colours, no shading, no gradients, no 3D, no gloss, no polish.
It should look like a person drew it quickly by hand, not like designed graphics.

## Canvas

- Horizontal 16:9, **exactly 1280x720**.
- Background: **pure white, filling the entire canvas right to all four edges and corners.**

**Deliver the thumbnail itself, NOT a picture of a thumbnail.** A generator asked for "a YouTube
thumbnail" will often draw a mockup: a white card with rounded corners floating on a dark
background, sometimes captioned "YouTube Thumbnail". That is wrong and unusable. Specifically:

- No dark or coloured area anywhere, including behind or around the artwork. The four corner
  pixels must be white.
- No rounded corners, no card, no drop shadow, no device frame, no browser or player chrome.
- No letterboxing or pillarboxing — the white must run to the edges, not sit inside bars.
- No caption, label, title bar or filename anywhere in the image. Do not write the words
  "YouTube" or "thumbnail" in it.
- Never grey, never coloured, never textured background.
- Leave a lot of empty white space. Two or three elements MAXIMUM.
- Draw everything BIG. Objects should nearly fill their half of the frame. If an element
  would be unreadable in a small box, it is too small or too detailed.

## Colour

- Black for all outlines and lettering.
- **Red is the accent, and it is used for exactly one job per thumbnail**: the danger, the
  consequence, or the question. Red equals signs, red question marks, red arrows, a red skull.
- One or two flat colours for the subject itself (blue, orange, grey, green, brown, yellow).
- Never more than three colours besides black and white.

## Composition — pick the ONE that fits the title

**A. The equation.** For a video about an ordinary thing being dangerous.
Left: the everyday object, drawn large and plainly. Centre: a big red equals sign. Right: the
consequence as a single stark symbol — a skull, a hospital bed, a grave, a flame. Nothing else.
Usually needs NO text at all: the equation is the whole hook.

**B. The character with an impossible goal.** For a video about building or achieving something.
A hand-lettered ALL-CAPS headline across the top — two or three words, ending in a question mark
if the goal sounds absurd. Below it a stick figure with a simple worried or puzzled face, holding
the one crude object they are starting from. A thought bubble containing the goal as a symbol,
with a small red question mark beside it.

**C. The single stark subject.** When neither fits. One enormous object dead centre with one red
mark on it — a cross, an arrow, a crack. Two or three words of hand-lettered caps at most.

## Text

- Hand-lettered ALL CAPS, thick and uneven, like marker on paper. Never a clean computer font.
- **Three words maximum.** Often zero. Never a sentence, never the full title.
- Spell it correctly and make it huge. If the text needs to be small to fit, cut words.
- No taglines, no channel name, no logo, no watermark, no subscribe button, no arrows pointing
  at nothing.

## Hard bans

- No photorealism, no stock-photo look, no cinematic lighting.
- No faces of real people, no celebrities, no brand logos or trademarks.
- No screaming shocked faces, no giant red circles round everything, no arrow spam — the
  restraint is what makes these read as confident rather than desperate.
- No borders or frames around the image.
- No small print. Nothing a viewer would have to squint at.

## Test before finishing

1. Are all four corners pure white, with the artwork running to the edges — no card, no bars,
   no caption anywhere? If not, redraw it as the thumbnail rather than a mockup of one.
2. Imagine it shrunk to a small box in a crowded feed. Can you tell what the video is about from
   the shapes and the one red mark alone? If not, make the subject bigger and remove an element.
