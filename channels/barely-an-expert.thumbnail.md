# Barely An Expert: thumbnail direction

Every thumbnail call, including retries, must load the actual pixels in `referenceStudy.thumbnailPaths`:
`thumbnails-1.jpg` and `thumbnails-2.jpg` (twelve of the source channel's real thumbnails) and
`thumbnail.jpg`. These are thumbnails, not video frames. The source draws its thumbnails differently
from its videos, so do not borrow the video frames' look. Attach them where supported; a CLI image
agent must open them before drawing. Stop and report it if they cannot be loaded.

Keep the source's layout and drawing economy while making original artwork for this video. Do not
copy a particular reference thumbnail's objects, arrangement, wording or people.

## Title band

- A white band across roughly the top third, with a curved or gently waving lower edge.
- The entire supplied title, verbatim, in black bold serif type with sharp classic serifs (similar to
  Alegreya Bold, not a rounded slab or display face), on two centred lines spanning most of the width.
  No outline, shadow or colour.
- Subjects below may overlap the band's edge but never cover a letter.

## Below the band — choose ONE

A. Three to five recognisable subjects side by side on a flat background, each one isolated and
   filling its share of the width: objects, artefacts, maps, documents, buildings or people. This is
   the source's most common layout.
B. One dense close-up of real landmarks and objects filling the frame edge to edge, when the topic
   is a single place or event.

No open landscape of sky, horizon, sea and clouds as a backdrop. No lone character standing in a scene.

## Background

One flat colour or a very gentle gradient, dark or muted: black, charcoal, dark slate, night navy,
olive, khaki or dark olive-brown. Light sky blue only when the subjects are a real place under open
sky. Never bright, cheerful daylight scenery by default.

## Drawing

Hand-drawn black outlines of medium, slightly uneven weight. Flat fills with one simple step of darker
cel shading, and a few interior lines where the real object has them: paper edges, wood grain, stone
cracks, windows, rigging. Recognisable real-world objects drawn with real detail, in muted true colours
(gold, brick red, slate, olive, paper beige) with at most one bright accent. Words other than the title
only where they belong to an object, such as a stamp or a map name, and sparingly.

No glossy highlights, glowing beams, soft airbrushed gradients, thin uniform vector outlines, or a
polished stock-illustration finish.

## People

Most thumbnails have none; prefer objects. When people are the subject, each one is an individual:
a round white head with hair, a beard, glasses, a hat or headwear, expressive brows and mouth, and thin
line limbs; or a normally proportioned figure such as a worker seen from behind. Never a blank dot-eyed
stick figure as a generic mascot, and never a sad face added as decoration.

## Check the output

Horizontal 16:9, 1280x720, full bleed. Compare beside the reference thumbnails at the same size: the
complete title, a curved band, a flat dark or muted field, isolated detailed subjects and hand-drawn
outlines. The title band alone does not make a style match.
No watermark, logo, channel name, player chrome, mockup, photo, 3D render or misleading claim.
