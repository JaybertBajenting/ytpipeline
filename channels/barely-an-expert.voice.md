# Barely An Expert: narration direction

Retain the existing voice `w7Ht0Z2E3mdProlicOSM`, model `eleven_v3`, and saved settings.
This correction changes writing and delivery guidance; it does not replace or clone a voice.
Saved transcripts demonstrate prose rhythm, not vocal timbre. Their roughly 143 words per
minute runtime average is a planning estimate; the configured voice has measured 150.1 words
per minute. Final audio determines duration.

Use a calm, clear, conversational American male read. Let sentence lengths and emphasis vary
naturally. Pause at meaning, allow ordinary breaths and pronounce names and numbers clearly.
An even register can contain mild curiosity and natural stress on an explanatory word.
Avoid trailer delivery, constant surprise, breathless sales energy and a robotic identical
drop after every sentence. Do not manufacture vocal imperfections.

The previous "never varies", "no interest" and "exactly the same tone" rules were overly
absolute. The old preview also encouraged fragments and a deliberately vague historical date.
Those instructions and that preview are superseded by this guide.

## Script rhythm

Read the five actual source transcripts listed in `referenceStudy.transcriptPaths`.
Use their connected explanatory prose as a structural reference, and verify facts separately.
Write predominantly in third person, with occasional inclusive language where natural.
An explanatory question can introduce a real question; avoid repetitive rhetorical hooks.
A brief direct thank-you belongs in the outro.

Aim for varied sentences averaging roughly 13-16 words across the whole script, including
shorter statements and longer 20-30 word explanations where useful. This is a guide, not a cap.
An image beat often contains one clause. Never add a full stop because the picture changes.
Concatenated beats must read as complete, flowing narration.

Write about 2,500-2,650 spoken words for the 16-19 minute format, then verify final duration.
Deepen evidence or explanations when a section is thin; do not repeat conclusions to fill time.
Use supported pronunciation fields for names; keep the narration readable and factual.

## Audio checks

Listen to a representative section containing names, a number and a longer sentence before the
full render. Confirm normal clause pauses, clear words, steady volume and a measured pace.
Check all ten chapter starts in the finished audio. Description timestamps must come from final
audio alignment and the assembled timeline, never word-count estimates. Keep music quiet enough
that every word stays clear. Configuration settings alone do not verify the finished soundtrack.
